About Vector Studioz
Process & Delivery
Retainers & Engagement
What is Vector Studioz?
Vector Studioz is an elite digital design and engineering studio. We partner with visionary founders and global enterprises to craft bespoke websites, 3D WebGL experiences, scalable design systems, and conversion-optimized digital products.
What core services does Vector Studioz provide?
Our capabilities span bespoke UI/UX design, Next.js and React full-stack development, interactive Three.js & WebGL 3D graphics, enterprise design systems, conversion rate optimization (CRO), and dedicated agile retainers.
What technology stack and frameworks do you use?
We design in Figma with rigorous component systems and design tokens. For development, we engineer with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Three.js, WebGL, GSAP animations, Webflow, and headless CMS platforms such as Sanity.
Who are your typical clients?
We collaborate with high-growth technology startups, venture-backed scaleups, creative agencies, and forward-thinking enterprises seeking category-defining digital flagships.
How fast can you deliver a completed website or design sprint?
Our agile sprints run in 2-week to 6-week milestones. High-converting landing pages can be delivered in as little as 10 to 14 days, while complex web applications and custom 3D WebGL experiences are delivered iteratively across progressive sprint cycles.
What does your sprint workflow look like?
We follow four structured phases: 1. Discovery & Strategy: Scope, user journeys, and wireframing. 2. UI/UX Design: High-fidelity Figma prototypes and interactive micro-interactions. 3. Engineering: Production-grade frontend and backend development with 60 FPS motion. 4. QA & Launch: Core Web Vitals profiling, Lighthouse 100/100 optimization, cross-browser audits, and deployment.
Who owns the intellectual property and code?
You do. Upon project delivery and milestone sign-off, you retain 100% intellectual property ownership of all Figma design files, custom 3D assets, animations, and source code repositories.
How do you handle revisions and feedback?
Collaboration is seamless. All reviews happen asynchronously via Figma comments and Loom screen recordings, with real-time Slack communication and scheduled sprint check-ins to ensure rapid iterations without bottlenecks.
How do dedicated agency retainers work?
Our monthly retainers embed dedicated senior designers and full-stack developers directly into your team. You gain on-demand design and development bandwidth for continuous feature releases, UI enhancements, and ongoing product scaling with zero hiring overhead.
How do we get started with a project?
Reach out through our Contact page to book a 30-minute discovery call. We will review your goals, scope the deliverables, and send a tailored proposal with sprint timelines within 48 hours.
What payment terms and structure do you offer?
For fixed-scope sprint projects, payments are milestone-based (typically 50% upfront and 50% upon final delivery and deployment). Retainers are billed on a flexible monthly subscription with no long-term lock-in.
Can you integrate with our existing engineering team?
Yes. We regularly work alongside internal product teams, engineering leads, and founders to accelerate delivery schedules, implement complex Three.js/WebGL features, or establish scalable design-to-code design systems.
