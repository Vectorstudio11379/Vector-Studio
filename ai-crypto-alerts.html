Is AI Crypto Alerts just another price alert bot?

No. Traditional crypto price alerts only tell you that a token moved.

AI Crypto Alerts tells you why it moved by combining:

  • Official posts & news
  • Security & listing events
  • Narrative & social signals
  • A Bullishness Score for each token & narrative
Can AI Crypto Alerts explain unusual pumps & dumps?

Yes. This is one of its main use-cases.

When a token pumps or dumps outside the market’s index or its sector range, AI Crypto Alerts:

  • Flags the move
  • Surfaces the most relevant signals
  • Provides an AI-written explanation of what likely drove it
Which sources does AI Crypto Alerts use?

AI Crypto Alerts focuses on InfoFi-grade data:

  • Official project accounts
  • Exchange announcements
  • Security & protocol notices
  • Curated crypto media & research sources
  • Key social and sentiment feeds

Everything is deduped and scored before it hits your feed.

Does AI Crypto Alerts provide financial advice?

No. AI Crypto Alerts is an information & insights tool, not a financial advisor. It helps you:

  • Discover, filter, and understand signals
  • Save time hunting for context
  • Make your own trading and investment decisions

You remain fully responsible for your trades and risk management.

How customizable are the alerts?

You can:

  • Build a custom crypto watchlist
  • Focus on specific tokens or narratives
  • Use feeds like Global Alerts, My Watchlist, and token pages
  • (Future) Use more granular filters for event types and threshold logic
How do I get started with AI Crypto Alerts?

Just:

  • Visit app.coingpt.tech/ai-crypto-alerts or open it inside the CoinGPT app
  • Create or refine your crypto watchlist
  • Start exploring the Global Alerts Feed and individual tokens
