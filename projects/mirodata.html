01 / OVERVIEW

Project Scope & Mission

MIRODATA serves enterprise analytics teams with distributed business intelligence. Vector Studioz engineered their secure front-end access portal featuring granular role-based access control (RBAC), SSO integration, and low-latency visualization widgets.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Strategic Roadblocks & Friction Points

The client needed a frictionless, enterprise-grade login and permission routing architecture meeting strict corporate compliance mandates.

03 / THE SOLUTION
