What is the CoinGPT Smart Contract Auditor?

The CoinGPT Smart Contract Auditor is an AI-powered smart contract auditor that runs in-depth smart contract security audits on Solidity code. It identifies vulnerabilities, logic flaws, gas inefficiencies, and standards issues, then produces a professional, shareable audit report.

How accurate is the AI smart contract security audit?

The auditor is trained on thousands of real contracts, known exploits, and best practices. It’s designed to surface:

  • Critical vulnerabilities
  • Risky logic and centralization issues
  • Gas and performance problems

For high-value protocols, you can use CoinGPT’s audit in combination with human review but for many projects, it provides a tier-1 baseline that rivals traditional smart contract audit companies at a fraction of the cost.

Will users, VCs, or exchanges accept this audit as credible?

Yes. The auditor produces transparent, verifiable audit report links:

  • Easy to share in docs, websites, and pitch materials
  • Structured with summaries, scores, and detailed findings
  • Clear enough for non-technical stakeholders to understand risk levels

Ultimately, each stakeholder decides their own policy, but the report is designed to match what savvy investors and exchanges expect from a smart contract audit service.

How long does a smart contract audit take with CoinGPT?
  • Light Audit (Quick Scan): typically minutes
  • Full Audit: usually under 2 hours for most contracts

This lets you run audits at every critical stage not just once before launch.

What does a smart contract audit cost with CoinGPT?

Pricing is credit-based and significantly lower than traditional audit firms, bringing the smart contract audit cost down by up to two orders of magnitude for many projects. Check inside the app or pricing page for the most up-to-date details.

Which chains and languages are supported?

The in-depth Smart Contract Auditor is focused on Solidity-based smart contracts on EVM chains such as:

  • Ethereum
  • BNB Chain
  • Arbitrum
  • Avalanche
  • Berachain

Additional environments and light-scan capabilities may be supported over time, but the core full audit experience is optimized for Solidity.

Does this replace manual audits completely?

For many smaller and mid-sized projects, the AI smart contract auditor can serve as a primary security layer. For high-TVL or systemically important protocols, teams often combine:

  • CoinGPT’s fast, repeatable AI audits
  • Selective manual reviews for critical components

Either way, CoinGPT helps you catch more issues earlier, reduce manual workload, and lower overall smart contract audit cost.

Can I run multiple audits during development?

Absolutely. The tool is designed so you can:

  • Audit early drafts
  • Re-audit after fixes
  • Run a final full audit before deployment

You’re encouraged to make security a continuous process, not a one-time checkbox.

How do I get started?
  • 1. Visit app.coingpt.tech/smart-contract-auditor
  • 2. Open the Smart Contract Auditor tool
  • 3. Choose Light or Full audit
  • 4. Provide a contract address, Solidity code, or GitHub repository
  • 5. Run your audit and review the report
