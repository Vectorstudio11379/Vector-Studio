Should our app be built in Flutter or React Native?

Both are outstanding modern platforms. We recommend Flutter for rich, custom visual designs and 120Hz animations, and React Native when your team already maintains an extensive React codebase.

Do you manage the entire Apple App Store and Google Play submission?

Yes. We handle app bundle creation, signing certificates, privacy manifests, store screenshots, metadata descriptions, and review correspondence until live approval.

Can the mobile app share databases with our existing web platform?

Yes. We architect clean REST and GraphQL API gateways that power both your web application and mobile apps from a unified source of truth.

How do you manage push notifications and deep linking?

We integrate Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and Apple Push Notification service (APNs), alongside universal links for smooth user onboarding and retention.
