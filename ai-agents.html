Have Another Question? →

What deliverables are included with a UI/UX project?

You receive complete Figma source files, reusable component libraries with auto-layout, interactive prototypes, design token documentation, and exported production assets.

How long does a typical web design sprint take?

A full flagship website design or product interface typically spans 3 to 6 weeks from initial discovery through final developer-ready design system delivery.

Can our internal engineering team build from your designs easily?

Yes. We structure all Figma files using clean auto-layout, standard 8pt spacing grids, semantic color tokens, and strict naming conventions aligned with modern React/Tailwind CSS components.

Do you also handle frontend engineering?

Yes. Vector Studioz offers end-to-end capabilities, seamlessly pairing our design team with our senior frontend engineering team.
