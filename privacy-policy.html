Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Vector Studioz collects when you visit vectorstudioz.uk, get in touch or work with us, why we collect it, and the choices and rights you have.

01Who we are

Vector Studioz (“Vector Studioz”, “we”, “us”) is a digital studio that designs and builds websites, WebGL 3D experiences, mobile apps, custom software and AI automation for clients worldwide.

For the personal data described in this policy, Vector Studioz is the controller. You can reach us about anything in this policy at contact@vectorstudioz.com.

When we build or run a product for a client and handle that client’s customer data on their behalf, we act as the client’s processor. In that case the client’s own privacy notice applies, and our handling of the data is governed by our contract with them.

02Personal data we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact and enquiry details: your name, email address, company, phone number (if you share it) and the content of your message when you email us, submit a project brief or use the contact form.
  • Project information: requirements, budgets, timelines, brand assets, content and account access you share with us so we can quote for or deliver a project.
  • Business and billing details: billing contact, company details and payment records needed to invoice you and keep our accounts.
  • Job applications: your CV, portfolio, links and any other information you send when applying to work with us.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: when you load our website, our hosting and security providers receive standard request data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested, referring page and the date and time of the request. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and diagnose faults.
  • Browser storage: the site stores a small number of preference and security values in your browser. These are explained in our Cookie Policy.

We do not currently use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking or third-party analytics on vectorstudioz.uk. If we introduce analytics in future, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

We do not knowingly collect special category data (such as health or religious information). Please do not send it to us.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

04Who we share data with

We share personal data only where needed for the purposes above, with:

  • Service providers who host and secure our website, store files, provide email and productivity tools, and handle payments and accounting. They act on our instructions and are bound by confidentiality and data protection terms.
  • Content delivery networks that serve fonts, scripts, images and video to your browser (for example Google Fonts, jsDelivr, unpkg, Amazon Web Services and the Webflow content network). These providers receive your IP address so they can deliver the file you requested.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants, lawyers and insurers, where reasonably necessary.
  • Authorities where we are required to by law, or to protect our rights, our clients or others.
  • A successor business if Vector Studioz is reorganised, merged or sold, subject to this policy.

05International transfers

We work with clients and providers in several countries, so your data may be processed outside the UK or the European Economic Area. Where that happens we rely on an adequacy decision or put appropriate safeguards in place, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for details of the safeguards that apply.

06How long we keep data

  • Enquiries that do not become projects: up to 24 months after our last contact, so we have context if you get back in touch.
  • Client and project records: for the length of the engagement and up to 6 years afterwards, to meet accounting, tax and legal requirements.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months after the role is filled, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or agree to us keeping them longer.
  • Server and security logs: kept by our providers for short periods, typically no longer than 90 days.

When data is no longer needed we delete it or anonymise it.

07How we protect data

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and two-factor authentication on the tools we use, least-privilege access to client systems, and removing project access when an engagement ends. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will notify you and the relevant regulator of a personal data breach where the law requires it.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted in certain circumstances;
  • restrict or object to our processing, including objecting at any time to direct marketing;
  • receive data you provided to us in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on consent, without affecting processing that already happened.

To use any of these rights, email contact@vectorstudioz.com. We will respond within one month and may need to confirm your identity first. There is normally no fee.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU it is the supervisory authority in your country.

09Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10Links to other websites

Our site links to client websites, project case studies and other third-party sites. We are not responsible for how those sites handle personal data, so please read their privacy notices.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services, tools or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will make that clear on this page and, where appropriate, contact you directly.

Questions about this policy? Email contact@vectorstudioz.com.
