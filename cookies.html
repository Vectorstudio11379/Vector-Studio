Cookie Policy

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on vectorstudioz.uk, what they do and how you can control them.

Last updated: 7 November 2026Vector Studioz

01The short version

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking or third-party analytics. The site only uses storage that is needed for it to work securely and to remember a few simple preferences.

02What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files a website places in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, let a site keep small pieces of information on your device. They can be first-party (set by the site you are visiting) or third-party (set by another service), and either last only for your visit (session) or for a set period (persistent).

In this policy “cookies” covers all of these.

03Cookies we use

Strictly necessary

These keep the site secure and working. Under UK and EU law they do not need consent.

NameProviderPurposeDuration
__cf_bm, cf_clearanceCloudflare (our security and delivery network)Tells genuine visitors apart from bots and protects the site from attacks. Only set when Cloudflare’s security checks need them.30 minutes to 1 year

Preferences

These remember choices you make, such as dismissing a banner or your cookie preferences. They are stored only in your browser and never sent to us.

NameTypePurposeDuration
consentMode, userCookieSettingLocal storage (first-party)Remembers your cookie preferences so we do not ask again.Until you clear your browser storage
Interface preferencesLocal or session storage (first-party)Remembers small interface choices, such as a closed announcement banner.Session, or until you clear your browser storage

Analytics and advertising

We do not use any. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.

04Content from third-party providers

To load quickly, the site fetches fonts, scripts, 3D models, images and video from content delivery networks, including Google Fonts, jsDelivr, unpkg, Amazon Web Services and the Webflow content network. These requests send your IP address and browser details to the provider so it can deliver the file, but they do not set tracking cookies on our behalf. Each provider handles that data under its own privacy policy.

05How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site from working, for example if a security check cannot complete. Guidance for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Safari
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge

06Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the cookies we use change. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. For more on how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy. Questions? Email contact@vectorstudioz.com.
