Careers F.A.Q.

Everything you need to know about joining Vector Studioz.
Have a question? Reach out to talent team.
What timezones does the team work in?

We are a 100% globally distributed team with core engineers across the Americas, Europe, and Asia. As long as you have a 2-hour daily overlap with UTC, you can work wherever you thrive.

How does payroll and contracting work internationally?

We process global salaries and contractor invoices via Deel in USD or your local currency with guaranteed bi-weekly payouts and compliant statutory contracts.

What is the paid micro-sprint stage?

Instead of abstract coding puzzles, you work on a scoped real-world challenge alongside our team for 3 days. We compensate you at your full consulting day rate regardless of the final outcome.

Can I take on personal side projects while at Vector Studioz?

Yes, absolutely. We encourage open-source contributions, digital art experiments, and side ventures provided they do not conflict directly with client confidentiality.
