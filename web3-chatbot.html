Will 3D WebGL make our website slow on mobile phones?

No. We apply Draco geometry compression, downscaled KTX2 textures, and dynamic resolution scaling to ensure consistent 60fps rendering across modern smartphones.

Can you work with our existing CAD or 3D product models?

Yes. We ingest STP, OBJ, FBX, and Blender files, optimizing polygon budgets and baking photorealistic PBR materials for real-time web performance.

What happens if a visitor has hardware acceleration turned off?

Our progressive enhancement pipeline detects WebGL support instantly. If unavailable, the site smoothly displays high-resolution WebP images or MP4 loops with zero downtime.

Can 3D canvas animations sync with normal page text scrolling?

Yes! We link Three.js camera coordinates directly with GSAP ScrollTrigger, creating fluid choreography between page text and 3D scenes.
