Is the CoinGPT Crypto Compliance Assistant a law firm or lawyer?

No. The Crypto Compliance Assistant is an AI-powered information and research tool, not a law firm or licensed attorney. It helps you understand crypto compliance topics and regulatory frameworks, but it does not provide formal legal advice. You should always consult a qualified lawyer before making legal decisions.

How accurate is the AI Crypto Compliance Assistant?

The Crypto Compliance Assistant is trained on up-to-date crypto and blockchain compliance data from regulators and official bodies. It aims to provide accurate, source-backed information, but no AI system is perfect. Use it as a research and scoping tool, then verify important matters with professional counsel.

Which jurisdictions does it cover?

The AI Crypto Compliance Assistant is designed to cover 100+ countries and regions, including major hubs like the EU, US, UK, Singapore, Switzerland, and more, with a roadmap toward 120+ jurisdictions. Coverage continues to expand as regulations evolve.

What kind of questions can I ask?

You can ask questions about:

  • Licensing and registration requirements
  • AML/KYC and travel rule obligations
  • Tax treatment of crypto activities
  • Securities and token classification frameworks
  • Cross-border and multi-jurisdictional risk considerations

The more clearly you specify your entity type and jurisdictions, the more tailored your answer will be.

Does the Crypto Compliance Assistant give definitive legal answers?

No. It provides informational, risk-oriented guidance and summaries of regulatory frameworks, not legally binding opinions. Use it to:

  • Understand the landscape and terminology
  • Identify key issues and questions
  • Prepare for more productive discussions with legal counsel
